Good Rentals / Living Victoria

A housing search service for newcomers arriving in Victoria, built and run remotely while navigating an inventory controlled entirely by real estate companies and private landlords, before applicants had even arrived in Canada.

My role Founder & Owner
Scope Brand, content, paid & organic media
Channels Website, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok
01

Challenge & Objective

Creating a housing search service for newcomers arriving in Victoria, operating remotely while navigating an inventory managed by real estate companies and private landlords who held the final decision-making power, all complicated by the fact that applicants had not yet arrived in Canada.

The goal was to build a relationship of trust with both applicants and property owners in order to successfully secure signed lease agreements.

02

My Role

Guiding clients unfamiliar with Victoria and its neighborhoods through regulatory, cultural, and financial aspects of renting, and professionally representing applicants to property owners or building managers during site visits.

03

Execution

Maintaining a consistent digital presence (website and print materials) to demonstrate professionalism and reliability. Creating and publishing valuable digital content across Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok. Coordinating and conducting visits to rental units while keeping cordial, professional relationships with property owners and constant communication between all parties.

04

Results

Successfully secured over 100 lease agreements through the housing search service, and generated highly positive word-of-mouth referrals driven by satisfied clients and digital content. A single 7-day Instagram campaign generated over 64,000 views and drove 1,279 visits to the website, on a final spend of CAD 35.

Learnings

Staying consistent with clients, dedicating the time and effort needed for high-quality, personalized service, and deliberately limiting the client base to a manageable size made it possible to maintain a level of service superior to that of competitors.
