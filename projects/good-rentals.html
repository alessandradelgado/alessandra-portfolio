Good Rentals / Living Victoria

A housing search service for newcomers arriving in Victoria — a relocation and rental-assistance brand built while navigating an inventory controlled entirely by real estate companies and private landlords.

My role Founder & Owner
Scope Brand, content, paid & organic media
Channels Website, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok
01

Challenge & Objective

Building trust with both clients and property owners, ensuring lease agreements were finalized within strict deadlines while meeting the expectations of both parties. Through effective tenant screening, personalized guidance, and proactive communication, I helped create a smooth rental process and achieve mutually satisfactory outcomes.

02

My Role

Guiding clients unfamiliar with Victoria and its neighborhoods through regulatory, cultural, and financial aspects of renting, and professionally representing applicants to property owners or building managers during site visits.

03

Execution

Maintaining a consistent digital presence (website and print materials) to demonstrate professionalism and reliability. Creating and publishing valuable digital content across social media platforms (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok). Coordinating and conducting visits to rental units while maintaining cordial, professional relationships with property owners and ensuring constant communication between all parties (applicants and landlords).

04

Results

Successfully secured 111 lease agreements through the housing search service. Generated highly positive word-of-mouth referrals driven by satisfied clients and digital content. Established a consistent and valued digital presence that resonated with the target audience, strengthened brand recognition, and fostered audience engagement across digital channels.
