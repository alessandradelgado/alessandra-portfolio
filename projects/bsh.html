BSH Home Appliances

Corporate-scale product and marketing management at BSH (Bosch/Coldex), connecting a large appliance portfolio to real commercial outcomes, from pricing and launches to e-commerce and competitive strategy.

My role Marketing Coordinator → Product Manager
Scope Up to 79 SKUs
Budget ~$110K CAD / year
01

Challenge & Objective

The main problem was a limited budget for small-appliance launch campaigns and market research, in a category facing strong competition.

The goal was to increase value share of the small appliance line in flagship stores, and to sustain food-prep products as the backbone of the business (the most profitable SKUs) across both direct sales channels and distributors.

02

My Role

Improving premium portfolio display, both in physical department stores and on online marketplace websites. Leading portfolio analysis and new SKU launches, while maintaining and improving profitability to grow market share (value) across all channels.

03

Execution

Strengthened premium perception by promoting a selected flagship range, and strengthened content and information (photos, videos, technical specifications) on retail websites. Led the launch of a new line of blenders and food processors with compelling technological features, aligned with a premium brand positioning.

04

Results

Replaced older blender models with a new, more cost-effective line that better appealed to the modern customer. Led the relaunch of the B2B e-commerce site for spare parts and accessories, which became a direct revenue stream for both the company and its retailers. Market share (value) in company-owned stores rose from 2.7% to 3.5%.

Learnings
