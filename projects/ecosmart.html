EcoSmart Commercial Cleaning

A local commercial cleaning company competing against national franchises, relying entirely on one paid channel that was starting to underperform, with no consistent presence anywhere else.

My role Digital Marketing Consultant
Timeframe 3 months
Channels Google Ads, Meta, Website
01

Challenge & Objective

The company was facing a decline in qualified leads and underperforming campaigns, while also needing its service differentiation and value proposition to be clearly reflected in its ads.

02

My Role

Rather than only tweaking the existing Google Ads account, I proposed strengthening high-potential campaigns through a diversification of ads, posts, and digital content, supported by a publication schedule organized around clear, predetermined objectives.

03

Execution

I restructured keyword targeting and ad copy, implemented new keyword analysis and location filtering for ad placements, and created additional visual assets. I also proposed diversifying beyond search by scheduling consistent posts across other social platforms (Meta).

04

Results

Over a period of three months, I significantly improved Google Ads performance: conversion rate increased 14×, cost per conversion decreased by 79%, CTR increased by 50%, and monthly costs decreased by 22%. Meta visits increased by 119%. Campaigns were focused solely on 12 neighborhoods in Victoria, BC.

Learnings
